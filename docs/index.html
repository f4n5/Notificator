what is a banwave? ⌵
a mass detection event where roblox anti-cheat flags and bans a large number of accounts at once. usually triggered by a new detection signature or heuristic.
how do i get notified? ⌵
enable browser notifications (click the bell icon / allow notifications when prompted). we push an alert the moment a banwave or update is detected. also — sign in with discord and configure the bot to get pings in your server.
are future updates guaranteed? ⌵
no — estimated dates are based on historical rollout patterns. treat them as a heads-up, not a promise.
how does the discord bot work? ⌵
the bot runs 24/7, polls roblox update endpoints and community banwave reports, then pushes messages to the channels you configure. sign in with discord, pick a server, and set your channel + role pings.
can i get banned for watching? ⌵
no. this is a public notification page. no account data is collected. you're just reading.
